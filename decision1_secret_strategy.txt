Decision 1: Secret Management Strategy

CHOICE: A (Platform environment variables)

REASONING:
For a containerized service deployed on GitHub Actions + Render/Railway, platform environment variables combined with GitHub Secrets are the industry standard and right choice at this scale.

Why Option A:
1. Simple setup - no infrastructure to maintain
2. GitHub Secrets are built-in, secure, and integrated with CI/CD
3. Secrets are masked in logs automatically
4. Perfect fit for single service with small team
5. Compatible with any deployment platform (Render, Railway, Fly, Heroku)
6. No operational burden of running a secret manager

Tradeoffs accepted:
1. No versioning/audit trail of secret changes (not critical for small team)
2. Manual rotation process (acceptable for this scale)
3. No automatic secret rotation (not a requirement yet)

For context:
- Service has ~5 secrets (DATABASE_URL, JWT_SECRET, API_KEY_A, API_KEY_B, LOG_LEVEL)
- Team size is small (1-2 people)
- No compliance requirements (no PCI/HIPAA/SOC2 yet)
- Scaling point: If we grow to 50+ engineers, we'd revisit for Vault or Secrets Manager

Decision recorded: 2026-08-08
